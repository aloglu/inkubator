Inkubator Design Direction
Inkubator · design direction · round 7

The whole app, desktop and mobile

A clickable prototype of every screen. Use the buttons above the frame, or click around inside it: the sidebar, cards, Edit and close all work. The phone screens are further down. All data is sample data.

New in round 7

  • Re-ink menu. Re-ink now means switching to a different ink. On the Desk it opens a short menu: the pen's most recent earlier inks first, then "Choose another ink…". See "Re-ink menu" above the frame.
  • Pen shelf, as a demo. Pens stay on cards, but "Shelf (demo)" shows the idea: pens laid in a tray, grouped by status, brand or nib.

Round 6

  • The shelf is the only ink view. Ink cards are gone on desktop and mobile. Inks are grouped by color family; filters and search still apply. Pens keep their cards.
  • Your nib is the app icon. It's shown on a cream tile, a dark tile and with no tile, with a flat tracing for 24px and below. It's in the sidebar too.
  • The swatch editor now matches the other editors. The linked ink is one field with a "Change" button, not a list of every ink.
  • Ink a pen, rebuilt to scale. It's a small dialog that reads as a sentence: [pen] → [ink]. Each side opens a search box with a few suggestions, so it never lists the whole collection, however large.
  • Mobile ink editor: the property scales now span the full width of the screen.
Changes from rounds 3 and 4
  • Photos. Cards always show a fixed frame (16:9 for pens, 4:3 for swatches). A photo is cropped into the frame around a focus point you set once in the editor. Detail views show the whole photo over a blurred fill, so nothing is cut off. Pens without a photo keep the drawing in the same frame, so a mixed grid still lines up.
  • Filters. Yes, they're needed. Each list has a Filter button that opens a panel of facets for that list, plus a Sort menu. Active filters show as removable chips with a result count. On a phone the panel is a bottom sheet.
  • No flush reminders. The Desk shows how long a pen has been inked, as plain information. Re-ink and Flush are equal buttons with no warning colors.
  • No resting-pen list on the Desk. It shows inked pens only.
  • Tighter "On paper" rows in the ink editor. Every step is the same width, so the rows line up and none has a stretched last option.
  • Lighting removed from the swatch editor.
  • Ink cards: no highlighted tags, a fixed property order with volume first, and "in pen" moved to the card's footer.
  • Icons in the tab bar and sidebar, using the Phosphor set the app already ships.
  • Pen status follows the app: Inked or Resting, set by whether the pen holds an ink. The editor no longer has a separate status control.

Desktop

App icon

Your nib works well as Inkubator's mark. It's simpler and flatter than the old painted icon, and its gold matches the app's accent. The burgundy band and breather hole give it a second color that nothing else in the interface uses.

Desktop icon: installers and docks need a background shape. I'd use the cream tile. It continues your previous icon, and it stands out on the dark docks and taskbars most people use. The dark tile works too if you'd rather it match the dark theme.

Small sizes: the painted nib holds from 32px up. At 24px and below the band and hole blur, so I traced a flat version in the same colors for the favicon and system tray.

In the app: the sidebar uses the nib on its own, with no tile, next to the wordmark.

On dark

Ink property order

Volume always comes first. After that, I ordered the properties by how much they shape your choice of ink and pen. The order follows the usual structure of ink reviews: what you'll see on the page, then how it behaves in the pen, then practical details. With ink cards gone, the order applies to the ink detail view (desktop and mobile) and the filter panel. The shelf itself shows only the swab, name and brand.

Mobile

Every screen at phone width. The tab bar holds Desk, Pens, Inks and More, with "Ink a pen" in the middle. Swatches, Stats, Activity and Settings live under More. Details open as full screens, and editors as full-screen sheets with Cancel and Save at the top. Scroll sideways to see them all.

Suggested order

  1. Shared pieces: the swab, the pen drawing, segmented controls, switches, the side sheet and the card footer. Every screen is built from these.
  2. Desk, pen cards and ink cards, including the new property order.
  3. Detail sheets and editors for pens, inks and swatches. The data model stays the same, apart from the lighting field, which the UI no longer shows.
  4. Settings and Activity: layout changes only.
  5. Stats: the rotation timeline and per-pen ink history are rebuilt from the "inked" and "flushed" entries in the activity log.
  6. Mobile: tab bar, full-screen details and editor sheets.
